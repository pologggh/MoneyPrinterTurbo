# Photosynthesis Process

Photosynthesis is the biological process by which green plants and photosynthetic organisms convert light energy into chemical energy.
Taking place inside chloroplasts, chlorophyll absorbs sunlight to split water molecules in light-dependent reactions, releasing oxygen as a byproduct.
The generated ATP and NADPH then power the Calvin cycle (light-independent reactions) in the stroma.
During the Calvin cycle, carbon dioxide is fixed by the enzyme RuBisCO and converted into glucose and energy-storing carbohydrates.
