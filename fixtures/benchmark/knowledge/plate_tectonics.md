# Plate Tectonics Theory

Plate tectonics explains the large-scale movement and deformation of Earth's lithosphere, which is fragmented into major and minor tectonic plates.
These rigid lithospheric plates float atop the ductile, semi-fluid asthenosphere driven by mantle convection currents and slab pull.
Interactions at plate boundaries produce major geological events: convergent boundaries cause subduction and mountain building, divergent boundaries create oceanic rifts, and transform boundaries generate earthquakes.
