# Black Hole Formation

Stellar-mass black holes form when massive stars exhaust their nuclear fuel at the end of their stellar lifecycle.
Without outward radiation pressure generated by nuclear fusion, gravity causes the stellar core to collapse catastrophically.
If the remaining core mass exceeds the Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff limit (approximately 2.17 solar masses), degenerate neutron pressure cannot halt the collapse.
The matter collapses to a gravitational singularity surrounded by an event horizon from which neither matter nor light can escape.
